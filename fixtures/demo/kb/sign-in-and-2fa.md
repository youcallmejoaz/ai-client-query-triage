## Lost two-factor device

If an admin loses the phone they use for two-factor authentication, another
admin on the same account can reset their 2FA under Settings → Users. If
there is no other admin, support resets it after verifying identity on a
short video call with photo ID. For security we never reset 2FA by email
alone.

## Locked accounts

After 5 failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes. It
unlocks automatically.

## Single sign-on

SAML single sign-on (Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace) is available
on Enterprise plans.
