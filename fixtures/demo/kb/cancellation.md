## Notice period

Subscriptions can be cancelled with 30 days' written notice before the
renewal date. Annual plans that are not cancelled renew automatically.

## Keeping your records

After cancellation, clients can export all payroll records (payslips,
reports, year-end documents) for 90 days. We keep statutory records for as
long as the law requires.

## Before you decide

The account manager can review pricing, seat counts and plan fit with the
client before renewal. Support staff should not offer discounts.
