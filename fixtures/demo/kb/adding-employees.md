## Add one employee

Go to People → Add employee and enter their name, start date, pay rate, tax
code and bank details. Tick "Invite to self-service" so they can see their
payslips online. New starters added before the payroll cut-off are included
in the next run automatically.

## Add many employees at once

Use People → Import and upload our CSV template. The import checks bank
details and tax codes before anything is saved.

## Plan limits

Starter covers up to 10 employees and Growth up to 100. Adding an employee
beyond the limit prompts the admin to upgrade.
