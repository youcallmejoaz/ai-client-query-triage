## Employees can't see their payslips

Payslips appear in the employee app once the payroll run is approved and
finalised, not when it is drafted. If a run is finalised and employees still
can't see payslips, check that they accepted their self-service invitation.
Admins can resend invitations under People → Self-service → Resend invite.

## Known issue: app version 4.2

Employees on employee app version 4.2 may see an empty payslip list. Updating
to version 4.3 or later from the App Store or Google Play fixes it.

## Questions from employees

We can only discuss payroll details with the employer's account admins.
Employees with questions about their pay should contact their employer's
payroll administrator.
