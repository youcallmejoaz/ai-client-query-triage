## When they are available

Year-end tax documents for employees (P60s) are generated automatically after
the last payroll run of the tax year and are available from 15 April. Admins
can download them in bulk under Reports → Year end.

## Employees who left

Leavers receive their P45 when their final pay is processed, not a P60.
