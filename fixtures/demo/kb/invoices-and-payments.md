## When invoices are issued

Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month and emailed to the billing
contact. Past invoices are under Settings → Billing → Invoices.

## Purchase order numbers

Clients can add a PO number under Settings → Billing → Invoice details. It
appears on all future invoices. Billing can reissue an existing invoice with
a PO number on request, usually within 1 business day.

## Payment methods

We accept direct debit (recommended) and card payments. Enterprise clients
can pay by bank transfer on 30-day terms.

## Changing the billing contact

An account admin can change the billing contact under Settings → Billing.
