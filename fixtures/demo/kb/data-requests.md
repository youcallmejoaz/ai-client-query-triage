## Our role

For payroll data we are the data processor and our client (the employer) is
the data controller. Data subject access requests from employees should go to
the employer. When an employer asks us for help with a request, our Data
Protection Officer handles it.

## Timelines

We send the employer the data they need within 10 calendar days, so they can
meet their one-month legal deadline.

## What support should do

Acknowledge the request, do not send any personal data by email, and pass
the request to the Data Protection Officer (dpo@tidewater.example) with the
client name and the date the request was received.
