## Where to check

Live status is at https://status.tidewater.example. Clients can subscribe to
email or SMS updates there.

## Incident on 22 September

On Tuesday 22 September the payroll app was unavailable from 09:10 to 11:40
UK time (2 hours 30 minutes) because of a database failover problem. No data
was lost and scheduled payments were not affected. A full incident report is
published on the status page.
