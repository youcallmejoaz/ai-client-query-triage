## Plans

- Starter: up to 10 employees, £6 per employee per month, minimum £30.
- Growth: up to 100 employees, £5 per employee per month.
- Enterprise: unlimited employees, custom pricing, named account manager.

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and the difference for the current month is
prorated on the next invoice. Moving from Starter to Growth keeps all data
and settings.

## Pricing for new clients

Prospective clients with more than 100 employees are quoted by the sales
team after a short call. Book one at https://tidewater.example/demo.
