## Annual plans

Annual plans can be cancelled for a full refund within 30 days of the start
date. After 30 days, annual plans are non-refundable, but the account
manager can move unused months to a different plan or entity.

## Monthly plans

Monthly plans are billed in advance and can be cancelled any time with 30
days' notice. We do not refund part-months.

## Duplicate or incorrect charges

If a client was charged twice or charged the wrong amount, Billing refunds the
difference in full to the original payment method within 5 business days of
confirming the error. Billing needs the invoice number(s) or the date and
amount of each charge.

## Service credits for outages

Enterprise plans include a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA. For each full hour of
downtime beyond the SLA we credit 10% of that month's subscription fee, up to
50%. Growth and Starter plans do not include SLA credits, but the account
manager can offer a goodwill credit. Credits are applied to the next invoice
and must be requested within 30 days of the incident.

## Who approves

Only Billing can approve refunds. Only account managers can approve goodwill
credits. Support staff should acknowledge the request and say who will
confirm and by when.
